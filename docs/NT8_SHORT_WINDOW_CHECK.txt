WHAT THIS DOES NOT ESTABLISH

a replay streamed 2026-08-21, that day has no independent reference to
compare against. To close the streaming gate the replayed day must be
one already covered by the capture or the full historical run - i.e.
replay 2026-08-19 or 2026-08-20 with days-to-load 2.
