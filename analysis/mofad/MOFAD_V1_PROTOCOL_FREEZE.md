### F12 — INVENTORY_TRANSITION_FLOW (session-transition inventory)
Mechanism: aggregated aggressor-flow imbalance accumulated in the illiquid
session acts as a dealer/inventory-pressure proxy that resolves as
continuation once RTH liquidity arrives (hedging/re-hedging of positions
absorbed against the overnight flow). Primary direction: **continuation of
the flow sign.** One declared direction per candidate; no inversion.

- **C-F12-1** (horizon T30): signal window = close-stamps (18:00, 24:00] of
  the prior calendar day ∪ (00:00, 09:00] of the entry day.
  Eligibility: ≥ 300 window bars, window volume > 0, entry bar present.
  Signal `R_on = Σ f_ofBarDelta / Σ f_ofTotalVolume` over the window;
  `R_on = 0` → skip. Direction = sign(R_on), **every eligible day**
  (no strength gate — feasibility: 256 eligible days).
- **C-F12-1b** (horizon T60): identical signal; second frozen horizon.
- **C-F12-2** (horizon T30): signal window = entry-day close-stamps
  (08:00, 09:29]; eligibility ≥ 60 bars, volume > 0. `R_pre` defined as
  `R_on`. Direction = sign(R_pre). (Flow-based; the dead LPCC arm was
  price-only — screen decision recorded.)

Entry (all F12): market at the **open of the first RTH bar**
(close-stamp 09:31). Stop distance = 1.5 × `f_atr` of the last completed
bar with stamp ≤ 09:30. Exit: open of the bar stamped entry+H
(T30 → 10:01 stamp; T60 → 10:31 stamp).

### F08 — IMPACT_ASYMMETRY (price impact and decay, bar-level)
Mechanism: side-split price impact per unit signed volume estimates which
side of the book is thinner; price drifts toward the low-resistance side.
Primary direction: **sign of the impact asymmetry.**

Definitions at evaluation stamp `m` (RTH, trailing window = the bars
stamped [m−59, m], require exactly 60 present):
`Δp_t = close_t − close_{t−1}` within the window;
`λ_buy = Σ(Δp_t | delta_t>0) / Σ(delta_t | delta_t>0)` (≥10 buy bars);
`λ_sell = Σ(Δp_t | delta_t<0) / Σ(delta_t | delta_t<0)` (≥10 sell bars);
`A = λ_buy − λ_sell`.
Conditioning: |A| ≥ type-7 Q75 of the pooled A-magnitudes from the prior
20 trading days' evaluation stamps (causal; ≥ 500 pool values required).
Direction = sign(A). Non-overlap: greedy scan; after an event at `m`, the
next eligible stamp is ≥ m+H+1. Eligible `m`: 631 ≤ m and m+1+H ≤ 960.

- **C-F08-1**: H = 15. — **C-F08-2**: H = 30.

Entry: open of bar m+1. Stop = 1.5 × `f_atr(m)`. Exit: open of bar m+1+H.
(Feasibility: 5,543 unconditioned T15 slots → ≈ 1,380 conditioned.)

## 3. Execution semantics (frozen, inherited)

Market orders only (no fill evidence exists for limits — inadmissible).
Stop race bar-by-bar from the entry bar; stop-first same-bar ambiguity
(stop wins). Gap-through stop fills at the worse of stop level and bar
open. Exit-bar rule: if the exact exit stamp is missing, the first later
bar's open; if none remains before 16:00, the day's last bar close
(logged). Missing entry bar → event skipped and logged. One contract,
non-compounded, MNQ $2/pt.
