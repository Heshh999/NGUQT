# NGUQT

## Reproducible Quantitative Research and Execution Platform

NGUQT is an end-to-end Python and C# research platform for auditing market
data, testing quantitative hypotheses, modeling realistic execution,
validating results against overfitting, capturing Level II order-flow data,
and deploying prospective strategies through NinjaTrader 8.

The system analyzes 2,503,622 MNQ one-minute records across 2,218 trading
days from July 2019 through August 2026. It includes 41 research modules,
32 test files, 51 findings reports, and 50 preregistered protocols or
frozen research specifications.
